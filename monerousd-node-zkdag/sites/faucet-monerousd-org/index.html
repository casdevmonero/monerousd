How it works

The faucet issues valueless test USDm on demand and records each note on the ordinary note path your wallet scans — no funding, no pool.

01

Bring your own seed

Create a wallet in the MoneroUSD app. Your post-quantum seed and scan key stay on your device — the faucet never sees them.

02

Paste your address

Copy your wallet's receive address and drop it in the box above. It's an ML-KEM encapsulation key — the faucet seals a confidential note to it.

03

Receive on-chain

The node records the note on the testnet chain. Your wallet scans it from the DA stream and the balance is yours to spend — real digital cash, on testnet.
